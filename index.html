Why Turkton

Built for the trades. Built for growth.

You didn't get into the trades to spend your evenings posting on social media or chasing Google reviews. You got in it because you're exceptional at what you do — and your reputation should reflect that.

Turkton was built for trades companies across Greater Vancouver who are great at their work but don't have the time, tools, or team to market themselves properly. We handle your entire marketing presence so you can focus on the jobs.

No complicated contracts. No guesswork. Just consistent, professional marketing that builds your reputation and keeps your phone ringing.

You're too busy

You're on the tools all day. Marketing falls to the bottom of the list — until a competitor with half your skill starts getting all the calls because they have 50 reviews and you have 8.

Reviews win jobs

80% of homeowners check Google reviews before calling a trades company. We make sure you have a steady stream of real, recent reviews that build trust before you even answer the phone.

Visible work = more work

Your best before/after jobs are sitting on your phone. We turn them into compelling content that reaches homeowners in your neighbourhood who need exactly what you do.

Past clients are gold
